# Focus Your Energy

1. **Assess Current Energy**: Sharp, steady, or fried (real-time self-report > energy profile > time-of-day heuristic).
2. **Classify Tasks by Cognitive Demand**:
   - Deep Work (creative, architecture, debugging) -> Peak energy.
   - Steady Work (reviews, editing, planning) -> Decent energy.
   - Low Drain (admin, filing, cleanup) -> Fried / dip window.
3. **Recommend ONE Clear Action**: Output `DO NOW`, `SAVE FOR LATER`, and `QUICK WINS` without decision fatigue.
