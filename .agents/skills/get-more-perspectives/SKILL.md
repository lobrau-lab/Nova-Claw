# Get More Perspectives (Advisory Panel)

1. Frame the decision in one sentence.
2. Lead with the **Executive Summary & Panel Recommendation** (Vote count, Consensus, Key condition).
3. Present 2-4 sentence positions from each advisor:
   - **Operator**: Execution feasibility & failure points.
   - **Skeptic**: Downside risk & hidden costs.
   - **Visionary**: Long-term upside & strategic moat.
   - **Customer Advocate**: Real user impact & willingness to pay.
   - **Finance Mind**: Unit economics, ROI, and burn rate.
