# LTX Video Prompt Architect

1. **One flowing paragraph, chronological**: Continuous prose from frame start to finish (4-8 sentences, under 200 words).
2. **Six Core Elements**: Shot scale & angle, single-source lighting & atmosphere, subject definition, sequential action verbs, camera trajectory with visual consequence, and dedicated synchronized audio sentence (Foley, ambient sound, quoted speech).
