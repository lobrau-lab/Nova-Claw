# Generate Fresh Ideas (5 Remix Lenses)

For any source material, generate 5 distinct angles:
1. **Contrarian Take**: Strongest defensible counter-argument.
2. **Personal Story Hook**: First-person lived-experience narrative angle.
3. **Data / Evidence Angle**: Concrete numbers, benchmarks, or case-study framing.
4. **Practical Playbook**: Tactical step-by-step guide executable today.
5. **What They Missed**: Second-order consequence or unexplored adjacent insight.
